# TM tray cell assets — provenance and decisions

Generated by `tools/assemble_tm_tray_assets.py` from `cells/tm_tray/asset_spec.yaml` into
`cells/tm_tray/assets/`.
Checked by `tests/test_tm_tray_assets.py` against the measurements recorded in
AI-Robotics-V2 D28–D30 (gripper, D455 case, jaws) and `cell_core.assets.JIG_BOXES`.

## Verified relations

- **tm_description link frames = v13 link frames.** Over 200 random
  configurations, links 0–5 agree to < 0.001 mm; link_6 differs by ≤ 0.13 mm /
  0.056°, which is the calibration in v13's joint_6. So tm_description's meshes
  attach to v13's links unchanged.
- **Gripper mount:** the gripper base frame is the flange frame turned
  `Rx(-90°)` (gripper −y = flange +z approach, gripper +z = flange +y up).
  Mounted on `flange`, not `link_6` (0.13 mm / 1 mrad apart).
- **v13's gripper still has tm5s.usd's stray mount offset** (D29: (0, +3.41,
  −1.76) mm in link_6). Its jaw joints are the egu50 joints plus
  (0.06, 3.78, −1.72) mm / (−0.19, 3.31, −1.79) mm. Therefore body, plate and
  D455 come from the D29-corrected egu50 meshes and the jaws hang on egu50's
  joints; only the jaw collider shapes (in the jaw link frames, which agree)
  come from v13. A test fails if the offset comes back.
- Jaw inner faces: gap = 2 (0.02565 + q) within 0.5 mm at q = 0, FIT, OPEN.

## Variants

- `tm5s_egu50.urdf` — **as installed (default)**: the gripper with v13's
  air-cylinder colliders (user 2026-11-12: the air cylinder is installed).
- `tm5s_egu50_no_air_cylinder.urdf` — the gripper validated in D29/D30.

The air-cylinder colliders are kept as exported, re-expressed in the gripper
frame. **Whether they carry tm5s.usd's stray mount offset is unknown** (they are
a separate prim from `gripper_body`); measure on the real gripper when
possible.

The push plate (flange x −0.041..0.037, z 0.117..0.124) can be reached by the
jaws: the left jaw touches it below q = 0.01575. The jaws are never fully closed
in operation (user), and `asset_spec.yaml: tool.operating_range` (min 0.021,
5 mm short of contact; width-fit 0.0413 and open 0.057675 inside) is what
planning uses; the URDF keeps the hardware limit. A test derives the contact
value from the meshes and fails if the range creeps into it.

## Tray

The tray is three convex boxes (two rails, recessed body), not one concave mesh:
MuJoCo and most CPU collision libraries take a mesh's convex hull, which would
fill the 2.5 mm recess the plate slides under (D17).
